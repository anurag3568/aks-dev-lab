End-to-End Digital Automation & Client Lifecycle

The AKS DEV LAB ecosystem is engineered to eliminate friction between initial order placement and final website deployment. By combining automated profile registration with secure payment gateways like Razorpay, clients transition from initial inquiry to active development seamlessly without administrative delays.

Step-by-Step Operational Lifecycle

01

Automated Onboarding

Clients submit store specifications, map pins, and package selections via the storefront order form. This instantly provisions secure client profiles and generates isolated password PINs (`phone.slice(-4)`).

02

Secure Verification & Payment

Clients log into their private dashboard portals to review order terms, clear setup fees via integrated Razorpay gateways, and download cyber-blue formatted digital invoices instantly.

03

Milestone Tracking & Dispatch

Administrators provide continuous progress transparency, pushing live milestone phase updates and status cards directly into the client's live notification feed and omnichannel channels.

04

Final Domain Handover

Upon completion, full domain control, admin console access, and zero-maintenance operational files are successfully transferred directly to the business owner.
